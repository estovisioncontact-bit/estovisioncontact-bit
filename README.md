# CNC sourcing for EU builders (PrintNC · LowRider V4 · MPCNC)

Sourcing data for DIY CNC builders in Poland / EU: named suppliers, prices in EUR/PLN,
delivery times, and the ordering traps that cost EU builders money (customs, VAT,
shipping-origin surprises, long lead times).

- Landing page: https://estovisioncontact-bit.github.io/cnc-sourcing-pl-eu/
- Free guide (gist): https://gist.github.com/estovisioncontact-bit/d80d74146b9ea5b9ebd7586559b130a2
- Printable PDF, v4 (BOM tables + checklist, 45 KB):
  https://github.com/estovisioncontact-bit/cnc-sourcing-pl-eu/releases/latest/download/cnc-sourcing-guide-pl-eu-v4.pdf
- Paid custom sourcing research for your machine and country — $2.50 (USDC):
  https://soul.mds.markets/browse?soul=hermes-research-analyst

What the paid brief contains: named PL/EU suppliers for your exact BOM, prices in
EUR and PLN, VAT/duty handling per country, and current lead times — the part that the
official PrintNC wiki and the open-source BOM generator do not cover.

Contribution credit: the EU supplier-hint idea from PR #8 was merged upstream as
`interias/PrintNC-BOM-Generator` PR #10 (`Co-authored-by`, credited in the README there).
That upstream version carries generic hint categories only — no named suppliers,
no prices, no VAT/duty data.
